## 7. Heuristic takeaways
1. **Type chain on your own nodes** (confirmed R1-R3, R6-R7). The auto clock goes house -> office -> pub (R-050, R-051), and every passenger in a building steps out at its own node each Clock step (R-054). So put next round's type on a different node of your line from this round's target: each delivery becomes next round's eligible passenger on your line. Office n20 -> pub n26 -> house n17 -> office n20 scored in R1-R3 with 1 bus and no Passengers action.
2. **Count targets first; passengers are rarely the bottleneck** (confirmed R4, R5). Deliveries are min(buses, eligible passengers, empty targets of the clock's type on the line) at the moment the marker resolves (R-060). In R4 I added 2 backup passengers but had one target, and P2 took it: 0. In R5 a single private pub plus a second Vrrooomm marker gave +2 with 1 bus. Rule: if targets <= buses, spend on Buildings, not Passengers.
3. **One bus + two Vrrooomm markers = two deliveries** (confirmed R5). Each marker uses all your buses (R-056, R-057). When Buses is gone, a second Vrrooomm marker plus a second private target substitutes for a bus that round.
4. **Never leave your line end at a node an opponent can reach** (confirmed R1 and R4, against me). An end there lets that seat take your last street via the shared-end exception (R-039). P0 in R1 and P2 in R4 both used my s39 from n17. Extend past contested nodes, or end at dead ends (n31) that no one else touches.
5. **Mid-line nodes on an opponent's snake are shared pools** (confirmed R7, R9). A passenger or empty building at a node that is on someone else's line (here n9, mid P2's line) is eligible for them too (R-058, R-059). Long snaking lines (P2, 15 streets) touch most nodes. Prefer to build targets at nodes no other line touches and that no other end is adjacent to.
6. **Last in turn order: first pick = the Vrrooomm slot that beats whoever shares your pool** (confirmed R1-R8). Single-space actions are usually gone by your turn. Take Vrrooomm first whenever a later picker with more buses (P0 with 3-4) shares your passengers or targets. Pick builds/lines with later picks; their letters resolve f..a anyway, so late = first for Lines/Buildings (R-030).
7. **Make your own targets after seeing Lines** (confirmed R3, R6). Lines resolve before Buildings (R-029), so a Buildings marker is a free look at where every line went this round. Build the target type only where no seat that still holds a Vrrooomm marker can reach it after this round's Lines, and only at a node with no passenger who would step into it at the Clock step (R-055).
8. **A waiting passenger at your target node kills that target** (confirmed R8, against me). At the Clock step the passenger at n31 stepped into my n31 pub (R-055). Before a round of that type, carry such passengers away first or plan for one fewer target.
9. **Forced extra builds land on future opponent lines** (confirmed R9, against me). The R6 "off-line" house at n22 became P2's target three rounds later, after P2 extended there. You must build every unit on the lowest open tier (R-034, R-047). Prefer types that will be useless when an opponent can reach them, or nodes far from every live end. Avoid nodes one street from an end held by a seat with many markers left.
10. **Plan your marker budget against the end** (confirmed). I used 4 markers in R5 and R6 for +4. That left me 1 marker in R8 and none in R9, while P2 scored 2 in R9. The game ends only when <= 1 seat has markers (R-066). A seat out of markers is skipped (R-027) but its buildings and passengers keep feeding others. When ahead, the last markers are worth more as Vrrooomm in later rounds than as setup, because opponents keep playing.
11. **Holding Clock as a cheap mandatory second pick** (speculative). When the mandatory second pick has no productive option, Clock costs nothing if you advance (R-051), and it removes the pause option from everyone else (R-050). In this game a pause never beat advancing for me. Bots never paused.
12. **Clear shared passengers first; keep private ones** (confirmed R6-R8). When choosing which eligible passenger to carry, take the ones on other lines (n26, n25, n17). Leave those only you can reach for your next Vrrooomm. When you are about to run out of markers, remove the passenger that sits on the strongest remaining opponent's line.
13. **Bot weaknesses to exploit** (confirmed). The PPO bot buys buses and Vrrooomm markers without targets (R3, R6: 3 wasted Vrrooomm). The gumbel bots rarely block and never pause. The rank bot (P2) aggressively uses the shared-end exception and mid-line passengers, so it is the one to guard against.
